Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Follow Up One ("we", "us") provides missed-call text-back for small businesses. When a call to our number, or to a business that uses Follow Up One, goes unanswered, we text the caller to help with their request. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • The phone number you called from, and the time of the call.
  • The text messages you send and receive in the conversation.
  • Details you choose to share, such as your name, the address where you need service, and what you need help with.
  • Whether you have opted out of texts.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and collect the details of your request.
  • To pass those details to the business you contacted, so a person there can follow up with you.
  • To run, secure, and improve the service, and to honor opt-out requests.
  • To comply with the law.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the business you contacted, and with the service providers that run Follow Up One for us: our phone and texting provider, the AI provider that helps write replies, and our hosting provider. They may use it only to provide those services to us. We do not sell your personal information.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Text messages and opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop texts from that number. You'll get one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. See our Terms.

How long we keep it

We keep conversation records only as long as needed to provide the service to the business you contacted, and we delete them on request.

Children

Follow Up One is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
