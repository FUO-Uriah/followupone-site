Follow Up One text messages

Program: Follow Up One missed-call text-back. When you call our number, or a business that uses Follow Up One, and the call isn't answered, you'll receive a text at the number you called from. The texts help with the request you called about: the assistant asks a few short questions and passes your details to the business so a person can follow up. We never send marketing or promotional texts.

How you opt in: by calling the number yourself. The recorded message on the call tells you a text is coming.

Message frequency: varies with your conversation, usually a few messages per request.

Message and data rates may apply.

Help: reply HELP, or email uriah@followupone.net.

Opting out: reply STOP at any time. You'll get one message confirming you've been opted out, and no further texts from that number. Reply START to opt back in.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

AI assistant: replies are written by an AI assistant for the business you contacted. It does not quote prices, confirm appointments, or promise arrival times; the business's staff handle those. Follow Up One is not an emergency service, and no person watches the conversations as they happen. If anyone is in danger, call 911.

Your information is handled as described in our Privacy Policy.
