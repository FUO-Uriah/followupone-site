About our text messages

If you call a Follow Up One number, or a business that uses Follow Up One, and the call isn't answered, you'll get a text at the number you called from about your request. We only text people who called first, and only about what they called about. We never send marketing texts.

How you opt in: by calling the number yourself. When we miss your call, you hear this recorded message before the call ends:

"Thanks for calling Follow Up One. If this is an emergency, please hang up and call 911. Sorry we missed your call. We'll send you a text message right now to follow up. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out."

Then you get one text confirming it:

"Hi, this is Follow Up One's virtual assistant, sorry we missed your call. How can we help you today? Msg & data rates may apply. Msg frequency varies. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out."

Businesses that use Follow Up One play the same message with their own name. The conversation continues only if you reply. Consent to texts is not a condition of any purchase.
